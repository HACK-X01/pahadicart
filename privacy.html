Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 11, 2026 | Effective Date: October 11, 2026
Your Privacy Matters: PahadiCart ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of consumers, local merchants, and delivery partners across Himachal Pradesh. We adhere strictly to Google Play Store User Data Policies and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act).

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines how Himora and Jeevanix collect, process, store, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile applications (Android APK/AAB), progressive web applications (PWA), and related web platforms (accessible at https://pahadicart.vercel.app).

2. Information We Collect

To provide high-efficiency 2-hour hyperlocal delivery in hilly terrains, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Identification Data: Full Name, Mobile Phone Number, and Optional Email Address provided during registration or checkout.
  • Delivery Address & Hill Terrain Notes: Street address, colony/sector, town (e.g. Solan, Shimla, Dharamshala), and specific staircase/landmark instructions (e.g. "Third floor via concrete stairs").
  • Precise Geospatial Location (GPS): With your explicit consent, foreground location data is collected to detect the nearest Vyapar Mandal merchant and optimize delivery routing along mountain corridors.
  • Transaction & Order Data: Items purchased, total bill amount, payment status (UPI verification status), delivery handover OTP, and delivery timestamps.
  • Technical & Device Data: Device model, operating system version, browser/WebView type, network status, and app performance logs to prevent crashes.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your data exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Facilitating hyperlocal food, grocery, and wellness deliveries from local merchants to your doorstep.
  • Calculating accurate delivery ETAs adjusted for mountain slopes and weather conditions.
  • Sending critical order status updates, kitchen progress alerts, and delivery OTP verifications.
  • Preventing fraud, verifying UPI payments, and maintaining regulatory audit trails.

4. Location Data Usage Disclosure (Google Play Compliance)

PahadiCart requests ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION permissions only when actively using the app (foreground):

  • For Customers: To accurately detect your location in winding hill roads without manual pin drops.
  • For Riders: To track transit progress and assist customer with live proximity radar during active delivery missions.
  • No Background Location Snooping: We do NOT track customer location in the background when the app is closed.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing & Protection

We NEVER SELL, RENT, OR TRADE your personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers. Data is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with:

  • Assigned Merchants & Delivery Riders: Only your name, delivery address, staircase note, phone number, and order items are shared with the merchant and assigned rider to fulfill your order.
  • Payment Gateways & Banking Partners: Encrypted transaction identifiers for UPI verification and merchant settlements.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Secure servers hosting SQLite databases with TLS encryption in transit and AES encryption at rest.

6. Data Security & Storage

All sensitive communications between your mobile device and our backend servers are secured using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS 1.3). Access to order records and user credentials is restricted through role-based access control (RBAC).

7. User Rights & Account Deletion Policy

In full compliance with Google Play Data Safety requirements, you have complete ownership of your data:

  • Right to Access: View all your active and past orders directly in the Customer App.
  • Right to Rectification: Update your saved addresses and profile information at any time.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): You may request complete deletion of your account and personal data at any time.
